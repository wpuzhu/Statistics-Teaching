TEACHING MATERIALS · ONE-PROPORTION Z-TEST

Interactive pages for a one-week module on hypothesis testing

These browser-based pages accompany a module on the foundations of hypothesis testing, taught through the one-proportion z-test. They run in any modern browser with no installation.

Binomial, CLT, and normal approximation

Pre-work and Lecture 1: the binomial model, the sampling distribution of the sample mean and of p̂, and when the normal approximation can be trusted (exact vs z).

One-proportion z-test explorer

Lecture 2, lab and homework: seven predict–move–explain activities on direction, sample size, the observed proportion, α, Type I and Type II errors, and power. A “Compare with previous setting” option keeps the last setting on the graph as a light grey curve, with a one-line note of what changed.

Open directly in the errors-and-power view: explorer.html#errors

Author: Puzhu (Chris) Wang, University of Michigan. All examples use invented teaching data.
